## 6. BUDGET TRACKING METRICS

### 6.1 Earned Value Management (EVM) Framework

**Baseline:** BRL 73.03M over 12 months

| Metric | Definition | Target | Frequency |
|--------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **PV** (Planned Value) | Cumulative baseline cost | Month-by-month | Weekly |
| **AC** (Actual Cost) | Invoiced + committed costs | ≤ PV | Weekly |
| **EV** (Earned Value) | Work completed % × BAC | = PV ± 5% | Weekly |
| **CV** (Cost Variance) | EV − AC | ≥ 0 (target ±2%) | Bi-weekly |
| **SV** (Schedule Variance) | EV − PV | ≥ 0 (target ±3%) | Bi-weekly |
| **CPI** (Cost Performance Index) | EV / AC | ≥ 0.95 | Monthly |
| **SPI** (Schedule Performance Index) | EV / PV | ≥ 0.95 | Monthly |

## 7. CHANGE ORDER PROCESS & COST CONTROL

### 7.1 Change Order Request (COR) Workflow

```
STEP 1: Identification
  ├─ Project Manager identifies change trigger
  └─ Document with photographs, sketches, scope clarification

STEP 2: Scope Assessment
  ├─ Classify change type: Design | Condition | Variation | Claim
  ├─ Estimate impacts: Duration, cost, quality, schedule
  └─ Review contract clause: Article X (variations, extras, claims)

STEP 3: Cost Estimation
  ├─ Obtain 3 quotes (if > BRL 50k)
  ├─ Apply SICRO rates for like-kind work
  ├─ Regional multipliers & contingency (5% for changes)
  └─ Generate cost proposal + schedule impact

STEP 4: Approval
  ├─ Client review & sign-off (≤ 15 business days)
  ├─ Financing approval (if contingency insufficient)
  └─ Issue formal change directive

STEP 5: Execution & Tracking
  ├─ Implement change
  ├─ Track actual cost vs. estimate
  ├─ Update EV baseline
  └─ Monthly reporting of change impacts

STEP 6: Invoicing & Close-out
  ├─ Invoice within 30 days of completion
  ├─ Retain documentation (RFIs, quotes, approvals)
  └─ Final reconciliation at project close-out
```

### 7.2 Change Order Authority Matrix

| Change Amount | Authority | Approval Time | Documentation |
|----------------|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| < BRL 50k | Site Manager | 5 business days | COR + 2 quotes |
| BRL 50k–BRL 500k | Project Director | 10 business days | COR + 3 quotes + schedule impact |
| BRL 500k–BRL 2M | Client + Contractor PM | 15 business days | Full impact analysis + alternatives |
| > BRL 2M | Steering Committee | 20 business days | Board-level approval + risk matrix |

### 7.3 Cost Control Procedures

**Weekly Site Controls:**
- Labor hours vs. planned productivity (target: 90%+ efficiency)
- Material waste tracking (target: ≤2% loss)
- Equipment utilization (target: ≥75% productive time)

**Monthly Project Controls:**
- Invoice reconciliation (±1% tolerance)
- Contingency consumption rate (should be ≤1% per month if on track)
- Supplier price variance tracking (flag >5% swings)
- Labor cost trends (track labor rates monthly)

**Mitigation Actions (if variance detected):**
- Cost ≥ 3% over: Reduce scope, defer non-critical items, re-plan schedule
- Schedule ≥ 10 days late: Increase crew, extend work days (if permitted), rent additional equipment
- Contingency burn > 2%/month: Escalate to steering committee for corrective action
